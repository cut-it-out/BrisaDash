# Brisa Café — Assumed Brand Palette

## Brand direction

**Personality:** Relaxed, sunny, welcoming, modern, and playful — not luxury or corporate.

**Inspiration:** Mediterranean turquoise water, deep ocean tones, sunlit sand, sunset accents, and whitewashed coastal cafés.

## Colors

| Color | Hex | Role | Suggested uses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Brisa Turquoise** | `#168F9C` | Primary | Branding, primary buttons, logo accents |
| **Deep Ocean** | `#174B64` | Secondary | Text, score HUD, UI outlines |
| **Sunlit Sand** | `#F3DFBB` | Background | Boardwalk, environment, warm backdrops |
| **Sunset Coral** | `#E87F63` | Accent | Coffee collectibles, highlights, celebratory effects |
| **Sea Salt White** | `#FCFAF4` | Surface | Menus, cards, overlays |

## Applying the palette in *Brisa Dash*

- Use **Turquoise** for the brand identity and primary call to action.
- Keep **Deep Ocean** behind light-colored text and for legible HUD elements.
- Use **Sunlit Sand** to make the coastal world feel warm and approachable.
- Reserve **Sunset Coral** for things players should notice immediately, such as coffee pickups or small reward effects.
- Use **Sea Salt White** for uncluttered pause and game-over panels.
